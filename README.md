What is this website?
This website is the official digital storefront for Crema Coffee House, a beloved local neighborhood cafe located on Larimer Street in Denver’s RiNo Arts District.

Its primary purpose is to inform visitors, showcase the experience, and drive in-person foot traffic to both the coffee shop and its sister market, the Crema Bodega.

What it does and who it's for
Acts as a Digital Front Door

Tells first-time visitors and locals exactly where Crema is, when it’s open, and what services are offered (dine-in and takeaway; no delivery).
Makes it effortless to get directions straight to the shop from a phone or computer.
Displays the Full In-House Offerings

Provides a transparent, up-to-date look at the coffee menu, specialty drinks, breakfast plates, hearty lunch sandwiches, and dietary options (like gluten-free and vegetarian items).
Introduces Both Concepts in One Place

Clearly separates and highlights the two distinct sides of the business:
Crema Coffee House: The daytime cafe focused on artisan espresso and scratch-made food (closes at 5 PM).
Crema Bodega: The adjacent market open later into the evening (8 AM – 8 PM) for natural wines, craft beers, pantry goods, and local snacks.
Builds Trust and Community Connection

Uses real customer reviews and authentic photography to give people an accurate feel for the neighborhood vibe, patio seating, and artisan food before they arrive.
